## What I'm Working On

### Master's Research — AI for Social Impact
Developing an integrated system to combat school dropout in Brazil:

- **Predict** — Identifying at-risk students using predictive models trained on large-scale educational microdata (Censo Escolar / INEP)
- **Understand** — Estimating the causal effect of public interventions (scholarships, transport, pedagogical support) through Uplift Modeling
- **Optimize** — Allocating resources via combinatorial optimization to maximize student retention under budget and capacity constraints

### Professional — Fintech & CRM at Méliuz
I lead Personalization at Méliuz, a Brazilian fintech with millions of active users. My work sits at the boundary between marketing and data science: I'm responsible for how the company communicates with its user base (who gets what message, when, and through which channel) but the decisions behind that are driven by models, not intuition.

Day-to-day, this means owning our lifecycle and retention strategy, designing segmentation frameworks that go beyond RFM heuristics, and running a structured experimentation program to measure what actually moves the needle.
